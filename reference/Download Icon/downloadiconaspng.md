## 🟥 Why icons in  PNG format:

### ✅ Best for static or image-based content

* Ideal for exports, presentations, social posts, or emails
* Better rendering in environments that don’t support SVG (e.g., some email clients, older apps)
* Preserves fixed pixel-perfect look for complex styles (e.g., Pop, Gradient, or Neon) where SVG size/complexity is high

### 🔁 Use when you need:

* A snapshot (e.g., exporting icons as bitmaps)
* Compatibility with tools that don’t support vector formats
* Simpler integration (just drop an image file)
